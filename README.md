# ☕ Cafe Business Performance Dashboard | Power BI

## 📊 Project Overview

The **Cafe Business Performance Dashboard** is an interactive data visualization project developed using Microsoft Power BI. It provides a comprehensive overview of cafe business performance by analyzing sales, revenue, gross profit, payment methods, and product-wise performance.

The dashboard transforms raw sales data into meaningful business insights, helping cafe owners and management understand business performance, identify profitable products, and make data-driven decisions.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall cafe sales and revenue performance.
- Monitor gross profit and profit margins.
- Identify top-performing food and beverage items.
- Understand customer payment preferences.
- Analyze revenue and profitability across different months.
- Track business growth through interactive KPIs.

## 📌 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard includes the following business metrics:

| KPI | Description |
|---|---|
| Average Order Value | Average revenue generated per order. |
| Total Revenue | Total sales revenue generated by the cafe. |
| Gross Profit | Revenue remaining after deducting food and labour costs. |
| Revenue Growth | Percentage growth in revenue. |
| Digital Payment % | Percentage of transactions made through digital payments. |
| Profit Margin | Percentage of revenue retained as profit. |

## 📈 Dashboard Features

### 1. Item-wise Gross Profit and Cumulative Profit
- Visualizes gross profit generated by individual menu items.
- Displays cumulative profit trends.

### 2. Sales Performance by Item Name
- Analyzes sales performance across different food and beverage categories.
- Helps compare individual product performance.

### 3. Cost Breakdown Analysis
- Displays the breakdown of revenue, food costs, and labour costs.
- Provides an overview of business expenses.

### 4. Revenue by Payment Mode
- Analyzes revenue generated through different payment methods.
- Includes UPI, Online, Card, and Cash transactions.

### 5. Gross Profit Overview by Item and Month
- Displays monthly gross profit for individual menu items.
- Helps identify monthly profitability trends.

### 6. Interactive Filters
- Enables users to explore business performance through interactive dashboard elements.
